# Messaging Framework

Build messaging from the audience, approved offer, real customer need, differentiator, and evidence in the locked brief. Do not fill a framework with invented facts or broad claims.

## Message foundation

### One-line description

[Brand] is a [specific category/offer] for [audience] in [market], helping them [approved benefit] through [real mechanism or proof].

### Positioning statement

For [audience] who need [verified need], [brand] offers [specific service/product/category] with [differentiator supported by evidence].

This is a working structure, not copy that should ship without user approval.

## Message ladder

| Message | Audience need | Supporting evidence | Approved channel/copy | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Primary message | [need] | [product/service fact] | [copy] | [approved / draft / open] |
| Supporting message | [need] | [evidence] | [copy] | [status] |

Use one primary message and a small number of supporting points. Prefer benefits the audience can recognize, but keep any implied result grounded in evidence.

## Audience adaptation

| Segment | Need and context | Relevant message | Tone | CTA, if supplied |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [segment] | [context] | [message] | [tone] | [approved action] |

Do not invent distinct audience segments or CTAs when the brief has not established them.

## Proof and claims

Separate:

- **Fact:** supplied/approved product or service detail
- **Benefit:** a reasonable customer outcome supported by that fact
- **Claim:** a statement that requires evidence, qualification, or legal review
- **Draft:** wording proposed for user approval

Flag comparative, health, environmental, financial, performance, pricing, and guarantee language for owner/legal review as appropriate. Never fabricate endorsements, metrics, awards, testimonials, certifications, or competitor comparisons.

## Test each message

1. Is the offer obvious?
2. Is the audience specific?
3. Can the brand substantiate the benefit?
4. Is it distinct from a competitor's generic claim?
5. Does it fit the approved voice and language?
6. Is it short enough for its actual placement?
