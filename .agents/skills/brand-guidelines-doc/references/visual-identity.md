# Visual Identity Guidelines

Use this file to document the approved system, not to impose a preset visual style. The identity's source of truth is the locked brief and approved asset files.

## What the system contains

Document only the components present in the project:

- Logo variants, exact filenames, contexts, and confirmed rules
- Color names, exact hex codes, roles, and tested combinations
- Type families, language/script coverage, hierarchy, and direction
- Graphic devices such as a grid, pattern, frame, illustration set, or motion behavior
- Photography and image-selection direction
- Materials, finishes, and production constraints when they are confirmed

## Make visual rules observable

Replace adjectives such as “premium”, “friendly”, or “modern” with visible behaviors. For example, document whether a page uses open or dense spacing, high or low contrast, a strict or flexible grid, close or distant crops, natural or constructed lighting, and restrained or expressive type. Tie each rule to a specific audience need or approved brand trait.

## Build a system, not a gallery

Use the [shared case-study library](../../shared-references/brand-design-reference-library.md) for examples of systems extending from a logo into packaging, digital use, campaigns, and guidelines. Study methods such as repetition, variant coding, rhythm, or logo-derived motifs. Never copy another project's exact mark, color palette, typeface, illustration, or distinctive layout.

Show each identity component on the touchpoints customers actually encounter. A mark alone cannot demonstrate behavior; select examples that test the important design decisions at different sizes and materials.

## Evaluation prompts

- Can a reviewer point to the approved source for each rule?
- Does the identity feel distinct for this audience and market?
- Does each component work alone and as a part of the system?
- Are scale, contrast, language, and use constraints documented?
- Are concept images clearly separated from approved assets?
- Can a different designer follow the guide without guessing?
