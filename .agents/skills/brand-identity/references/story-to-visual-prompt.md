# Brand Story to Visual Prompt

Use this after the story and the prompt direction have been confirmed in the brand intake. The goal is to make a visual prompt from the user's real story, not to invent a more dramatic story for the image generator.

## Extract the story inputs

Record only confirmed material:

- **Source story:** exact approved founder, product, ingredient, craft, place, or customer story.
- **Factual anchors:** the concrete objects, gestures, settings, and details the source actually supports.
- **Audience and feeling:** what the intended audience should recognize or feel, in the language they use.
- **Claim boundary:** claims or cultural associations that must not be implied.
- **Visual translation:** one visual metaphor or sequence derived from an anchor, plus why it fits.
- **Use:** poster, hero visual, campaign image, presentation chapter, packaging illustration, or another user-selected application.

If no story is provided, ask whether the user wants to develop one. Do not fabricate heritage, founder history, craft traditions, ingredient origin, community rituals, or impact claims.

## Prompt pattern

```text
Create [specific selected visual deliverable] for [brand] using the attached approved brand brief and logo reference.

Story source (approved wording/facts): [quote or accurately summarize the user's approved story].
Audience and market: [confirmed audience, country/region, language/dialect].
Emotional intent: [one user-approved feeling or response].
Visual idea: [one concrete scene/metaphor derived from a factual anchor], because [brief-grounded reason].
Composition: [focal point, hierarchy, negative space for exact copy/logo, crop and intended placement].
Visual treatment: [confirmed illustration/photography/rendering technique, material, light, camera distance, contrast and texture].
Palette: use these exact approved hex values and roles only: [#HEX role, #HEX role...].
Copy: [exact approved copy], or leave [specified region] empty for manual typesetting. Do not invent words or misspell Arabic.
Logo: preserve the attached approved asset exactly. If fidelity cannot be maintained, leave a clean unbranded area for manual compositing.
Format: [aspect ratio, pixel dimensions if verified, orientation, safe areas].
Avoid: [unsupported cultural symbols, generic regional shorthand, invented story claims, fake text, extra logo, unrelated props, watermark].
Review: check story fidelity, exact script, palette, crop, audience fit, and that no new factual claim has appeared.
```

## Review before handoff

1. Trace every depicted story detail back to a confirmed source.
2. Remove symbols whose meaning is assumed from country or religion alone.
3. Keep visual storytelling distinct from facts: a metaphor must not imply an unapproved product benefit.
4. For posters, banners, and presentation covers, reserve clear space for editable copy and the actual logo.
5. Label the prompt as concept imagery when the generated scene is illustrative rather than documentary.
